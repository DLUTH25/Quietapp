Quiet

Post to TikTok without the numbers.

Quiet is a minimal iPhone app for posting videos to TikTok. You pick a video, write a caption, choose who can see it, and post. Quiet has no feed and never shows views, likes, comments or follower counts, so you can share your work and get back to your day.

Quiet uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.
