Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Quiet is built to collect as little as possible. There is no Quiet server, no account, no analytics and no ads.

What Quiet accesses

  • Your TikTok sign-in. When you connect TikTok, Quiet receives access tokens that let it post on your behalf. They are stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain.
  • Basic TikTok profile info. Your display name and the posting options TikTok allows for your account (such as privacy choices and maximum video length), so the app can show "Posting as" and the right choices.
  • The video you choose. Only the video you pick in the photo picker, plus the caption and settings you enter. They are sent directly from your device to TikTok.

What Quiet does not access

Quiet does not request your followers, following list, likes, views, comments, messages, other videos or analytics. It does not access the rest of your photo library.

Sharing

Your video, caption and post settings go only to TikTok, to publish them. Quiet's developer never receives them. TikTok's handling of your content is covered by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Tap Disconnect TikTok in Quiet's Settings to revoke access and delete the stored tokens. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored. Videos you posted stay on TikTok until you delete them there.

Children

Quiet is not intended for anyone under the minimum age TikTok requires.
